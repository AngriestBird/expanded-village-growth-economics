# Goal stats and interpretation

EVGE uses standard OpenTTD Goal statistics to make performance easy to check from the company window.

## Growth points

Each time a town exceeds its historical maximum population, the contributor for that town gets points for the added population.

- This is a proxy for how well a company supports long-term growth.
- The number is not a bonus, it is a scoreboard metric.

## Average town categories

The average number of required categories across your monitored towns.

- Higher values mean you are supporting more complex towns.
- Low values often mean mostly basic passenger/mail supply.

## Contributed towns and stagnation

- **Contributed towns** counts towns you are currently contributing to.
- **Not growing towns** are currently under stop-growth conditions.

## Largest town and fastest growth

- **Biggest town** tracks your largest population hit.
- **Fastest growing town in days** shows best expansion cadence among your contributions.

Use these values to tune where to invest in rail, road, and industry placement.
